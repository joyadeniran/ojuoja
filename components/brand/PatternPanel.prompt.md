One-line: the striped brand field — reach for it when a section needs to read as unmistakably Ojuoja rather than as a plain white block.

```jsx
<PatternPanel variant="lime">
  <h1>Your Next Craving is Just Around the Corner.</h1>
</PatternPanel>
```

Use `lime` behind dark green headline type, `green` behind white type or a cut-out product photo, `market` only as a decorative promo backdrop with a solid block laid over it. Never stack two pattern panels in a row.
