One-line: every clickable call to action on Ojuoja; the badge circle is what makes it look like Ojuoja rather than generic commerce.

```jsx
<Button badgeIcon="shopping-basket">Shop Now!</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">Become a Vendor</Button>
<Button variant="onbrand" badgeIcon="arrow-right">Explore Category</Button>
```

Use the badge on hero and section CTAs, not on form submits or in dense tables. One primary per view. `onbrand` is the only variant allowed on a green field.
