One-line: the unit of the catalogue — use it in any product grid.

```jsx
<ProductCard name="Fresh sweet corn (5 pcs)" price={1800} was={2200} vendor="Mama T Stores" verified rating={4.8}
  image="assets/photography/prod-corn.png" flag={{ label: "-18%" }} onAdd={add} />
```

Prices are always naira with the ₦ sign and no decimals. Grid at 4 across on desktop with `gap: var(--space-8)`. Photos are square, product-on-white or product-on-lime.
